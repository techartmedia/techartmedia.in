## 🌟 Overview

This is the production-grade, responsive official website for **TechArtMedia**, engineered for high lead conversion, swift page loading, and seamless client acquisition across India and internationally.

### Key Highlights
- **Branding & Visual Hierarchy**: Built using authentic TechArtMedia high-resolution brand assets, gradients (Orange `#f97316`, Sky Blue `#0284c7`, Emerald `#10b981`), and dark-mode glassmorphism.
- **8 Core Services**: Detailed breakdown of Graphic Design, Web Design & Dev, Social Media Management, Digital Marketing & Ads, Video Editing & Reels, Software Development, Print Design, and Corporate Branding.
- **Official Social Media Packages**: Exact plans directly from the agency price flyer:
  - **Starter PRESENCE**: ₹15,000 / month (9 Posts, 3 Reels, 1 Model Reel, Bio revamp)
  - **Growth & ENQUIRIES**: ₹25,000 / month (16 Posts, 5 Reels, 3 Model Reels, Meta Ads setup & management)
  - **Premium GROWTH**: ₹35,000 / month (22 Posts, 8 Reels, 5 Model Reels, Advanced ads & weekly lead audits)
  - Monthly vs. Quarterly toggle with 10% discount dynamic calculation.
- **Interactive Project & ROI Estimator**: Allows prospective clients to customize reel counts, add landing pages or ad campaigns, and immediately tap to dispatch an itemized proposal to WhatsApp.
- **Founder & Leadership Spotlight**: Spotlights Durvesh Bhavsar with an executive vision statement and direct communication links.
- **Interactive Contact Funnel**: Validated form with automatic WhatsApp pre-formatted message dispatch and local lead backup.
- **Zero-Dependency Lightweight Build**: Powered by Tailwind CSS CDN, Lucide Icons, and Vanilla JavaScript.
