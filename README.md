## 📸 Updating Founder Photo

When you are ready to upload your photo:
1. Save your photo as **`founder.jpg`**.
2. Replace the file at:
   ```
   C:\Users\Admin\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\techartmedia-website\assets\founder.jpg
   ```
3. Both the Home and About pages will automatically display your picture!
